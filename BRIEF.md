## Rules (same as before)
- Sources in order: Wikimedia Commons (proper User-Agent), the venue's own site or press page,
  Flickr under a Creative Commons license. No watermarks, no unlicensed stock, no AI images.
- Landscape crops. Day-page slots are ~1.9:1; opener tiles are ~1.25:1. Subject near center.
- At least 1200px wide, under 400 KB each. Resize and compress.
- One credit line per image in each folder's `CREDITS.txt`: `<n>.jpg — <URL> — <author> — <license>`.
- This is a private gift, not for publication, so venue press photos are acceptable where
  Commons has nothing. Prefer Commons when it has a good one.
- If a place has no usable photo anywhere, leave that slot empty (the build shows a colored
  plate) and say so in the summary. Don't substitute a different place.
- Don't edit `build.py`, `style.css` or any copy.
